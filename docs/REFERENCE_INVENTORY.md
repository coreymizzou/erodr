## Written historical evidence

### Erodr press release, 2014-08-26

Source: [PR Newswire — “Erodr App for College Students Graduates from Stealth, Goes National”](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/erodr-app-for-college-students-graduates-from-stealth-goes-national-272703071.html)

- Pilot campuses: Mizzou, Missouri State, Miami University (Ohio), Virginia Tech, and James Madison University.
- Roughly half of early posts included photos; around 15% used anonymous posting.
- Anonymous posts revealed only gender and collected private responses.
- Posts expired; peer thumbs-up/down voting determined post life.
- `Anonymous Ticker` showed anonymous posts from colleges nationwide.
- Validated students gained a school `Classmates` feed and a `National Feed`.
- Validation used a `.edu` email.

### Silicon Prairie News profile, 2013-10-21

Source: [“Social media app erodr establishes itself at Mizzou, expands to 15 schools”](https://siliconprairienews.com/2013/10/social-media-app-erodr-establishes-itself-at-mizzou-expands-to-15-schools/)

- Users were called `rodies` (pronounced “roadies”).
- The `streamer` showed posts from rodies within a chosen distance range.
- Users could set a post time limit; likes and dislikes affected remaining life.
- Posts could be anonymous and filtered by categories including gender.
- Profiles required school email, name, and photo.
