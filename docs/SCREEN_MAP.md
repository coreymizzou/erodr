# Erodr Screen Map

The firsthand 2013–2015 iPhone captures in `reference/user-supplied/` are the primary visual specification. Later screenshots and written descriptions remain supporting evidence.

## 1. Streamer feed — directly observed

- **References:** `1NFJTtrL.png`, `ucuYrCqQ.png`, `TM-J6Oww.png`.
- **Layout:** white header with green graduation cap, centered thin lowercase `erodr`, and outline bell; Popular/Recent/Nearest selector; gray feed gutter; white cards with a thin green outline; labeled five-tab bottom bar.
- **Hierarchy:** circular avatar → bold author or `Anonymous Male` → Mizzou → location and clock/age → media/body → score, up/down chevrons, and response count.
- **Controls:** stream chooser, notifications, sort, vote, open responses, and Streamer/Map/Post/Chat/Profile tabs.
- **Destinations:** post detail, stream chooser, notifications, profile, composer, chat, and map.
- **Unresolved:** exact Nearest radius query, trophy threshold, and whether the radar appears during refresh or is a persistent loading affordance.

## 2. Profile — directly observed

- **References:** `tbcye2CA.png`, `6iQD4azA.png`.
- **Layout:** white navigation with green Done and optional Report Abuse; gray identity hero; large circular avatar; name/Mizzou; horizontal photo strip; optional green Validated mark; centered italic bio; Rodie tenure and likes; connections; Start Chat/Connect/Block actions; attributed activity below.
- **Privacy:** anonymous posts never appear as public profile activity.
- **Unresolved:** own-profile editing entry, photo gallery interaction, validation flow, and whether action placement changes with connection state.

## 3. Splash / branding — asset observed, screen inferred

- **References:** `Erodr-logo.png`, `images.jpeg`.
- **Layout:** prototype uses the supplied green radar artwork on black, followed by a period-compatible login using the supplied rounded-square app icon.
- **Control/destination:** automatic transition or tap to login.
- **Unresolved:** original splash duration, background, loading behavior, and exact login placement.

## 4. Login and college verification — required, visually undocumented

- **References:** supplied app icon plus written evidence for `.edu` validation.
- **Controls:** `.edu` email, demo login, continue, account creation/verification.
- **Destination:** Mizzou Streamer or college verification.
- **Unresolved:** original copy and authentication sequence.

## 5. Post composer — historically supported, visually undocumented

- **References:** 2013 descriptions of time/distance/anonymity and 2014 descriptions of photos.
- **Layout:** flat full-screen composer with text, image, identity, audience/range, and lifespan controls.
- **Controls:** identified/anonymous, Mizzou Streamer audience, photo attachment, time/range, publish/cancel.
- **Unresolved:** original slider appearance, labels, defaults, and whether gender was explicitly chosen or inherited from profile.

## 6. Public responses — partially observed

- **References:** comment icon/count throughout the feed captures; written evidence for public and private responses.
- **Layout:** selected historical post card, dense response list, and response composer.
- **Controls:** identified/anonymous public response and private response where supported.
- **Unresolved:** era-specific `Comments` versus `Responses` heading and vote controls on responses.

- **Unresolved:** participant aliases, source-post retention, photo-message UI, and conversation-row design.

## 8. Map — tab directly observed, screen inferred

- **Prototype:** simulated Mizzou coordinates, post radius, roads, and range rings.
- **Unresolved:** original map provider, pin art, filters, clustering, and post-preview treatment.
